RECOMMENDED FOR YOUR WORKFLOW

Nanobrowser · Chrome side panel

It works in your own browser session and asks before critical actions. Neural Ant prepares the search task and stores results you import; the extension runs separately.

Get Nanobrowser ↗

1 · Copy your search task

Paste into Nanobrowser

Before the first search: install the Chrome extension, configure your chosen model/API key in its settings, open the side panel, start a task, and paste the copied instructions. The prompt asks the agent to search and collect evidence only. It must not submit applications, upload your resume, or bypass access controls.

Nanobrowser runs locally, but page content you ask it to analyze may be sent to the LLM provider configured in its settings. Use your own trusted provider and review its privacy settings.

2 · Add the results here

JSON from your agent

Paste the JSON array returned by Nanobrowser. Neural Ant will save these leads in this browser and show the evidence fields.

Check each source and sponsorship statement yourself. Employer filing history does not prove sponsorship for a specific opening.
